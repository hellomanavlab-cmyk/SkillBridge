1. Introduction and acceptance of terms

These Terms and Conditions (the “Terms”) govern your access to and use of the SkillBridge platform, including the SkillBridge website and any related pages (together, the “Platform”). SkillBridge is powered by ManavLab.

By accessing or using the Platform, you confirm that you have read, understood and agreed to these Terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform.

If you use the Platform on behalf of a business, you confirm that you are authorised to accept these Terms for that business.

2. SkillBridge platform purpose

SkillBridge connects businesses, clients and freelancers. It is a business discovery and collaboration platform. It helps people find relevant businesses and freelancers, and helps businesses and clients make enquiries and begin conversations.

Basic business and freelancer listings are free. Premium membership and certain research services are separate paid services, as described in these Terms and in the applicable policies.

3. Platform role and limitations

SkillBridge provides the Platform as a discovery and introduction service. SkillBridge is not the employer, client, agent or partner of any business or freelancer listed on the Platform, unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

SkillBridge does not control, and is not responsible for, the quality, legality, timing or outcome of any work agreed between users. SkillBridge does not guarantee sales, projects, meetings, conversions or revenue.

Initial operations are manual and subject to review and approval by SkillBridge. Some features described in these Terms or related policies may be planned rather than currently available. Planned features are not live until SkillBridge confirms they are available on the Platform.

4. Client responsibilities

If you are a client (a person or business looking for a product, service or provider), you agree to:

  • provide accurate and complete information in any enquiry or requirement you submit;
  • submit requirements only if you genuinely wish to be connected with a relevant provider;
  • communicate respectfully with businesses and freelancers, and not send spam or unsolicited messages;
  • make your own checks before entering into any agreement or making any payment to a provider.

5. Business owner responsibilities

If you list a business on the Platform, you confirm and agree that:

  • you are the business owner or an authorised representative of the business;
  • all information you provide, including business name, category, location, products or services, website and social links, is accurate and up to date;
  • you have the right to publish the business name, logo, images and any other content you submit;
  • you will not publish public phone numbers or email addresses unless you have given separate permission for them to be shown;
  • you will keep your listing updated and tell SkillBridge promptly about any changes or errors.

6. Freelancer responsibilities

If you create a freelancer profile on the Platform, you confirm and agree that:

  • the name, contact details, skills, services, experience and portfolio you provide are accurate and belong to you or you have the right to use them;
  • your profile does not claim qualifications, certifications, clients, awards or results that you do not hold or cannot support;
  • you will not use the Platform to misrepresent your identity, skills or availability;
  • you will keep your profile updated and tell SkillBridge promptly about any changes.

7. Listing submission and approval

Listings and profiles are submitted by users and reviewed by SkillBridge before they are published. SkillBridge may approve, reject, edit for clarity, pause or remove any listing or profile at its discretion, including where information appears inaccurate, incomplete, duplicated or misleading.

A listing or profile that is published has been reviewed for basic presentation. Published does not mean verified. Please read the Business and Freelancer Listing Policy (SB-LP-03) for the meaning of each listing status.

8. Accuracy of information

You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide to SkillBridge and to other users. SkillBridge relies on information supplied by users and, where stated, on publicly available sources. SkillBridge does not guarantee that all information on the Platform is complete, current or error-free.

If you become aware that information relating to you or your business is wrong, please contact SkillBridge at skillbridge.manavlab@gmail.com so that it can be corrected.

9. Business and freelancer connections

SkillBridge may introduce a client to a relevant business or freelancer, or make a listing visible to a relevant audience. Any connection is made only where the relevant parties have agreed, or where the information is already published with permission.

Private contact details are not shared without the permission of the person concerned. Public contact details are shown only where the owner has given separate permission. Please read the Privacy Policy (SB-PP-02) for the consent rules that apply.

Once users are connected, the relationship and any agreement between them is between those users. SkillBridge is not a party to that agreement.

10. Enquiries, quotations and appointments

An enquiry sent through the Platform is a request for information or connection. It is not a binding order or booking unless SkillBridge or the relevant provider confirms it in writing.

Quotations, proposals and appointments are made between the client and the relevant provider, or between the client and SkillBridge where SkillBridge is providing a paid service. Any quotation is valid only for the period and scope stated in it.

SkillBridge does not guarantee that any enquiry will receive a reply, that any appointment will take place, or that any quotation will be accepted.

11. Lead research services

SkillBridge may offer lead research services. These currently include Raw Business Leads and Verified Research Leads, as described in the Lead Generation Service Policy (SB-LG-04). Raw Business Leads are free. Verified Research Leads are charged at ₹699 for 10 leads, and 15 and 25 leads are subject to a Custom Quote.

A Raw Business Lead is a research-based prospect. It is not a confirmed interested buyer. A Verified Research Lead is a research-based report, not a guaranteed customer.

Research outputs distinguish confirmed facts from research-based observations. SkillBridge does not guarantee the accuracy or completeness of research data, and does not guarantee any sales, meetings or conversions.

12. Premium membership conditions

Premium membership is a separate paid service. Basic business and freelancer listings are free. Premium does not automatically mean verified.

The features, benefits and any charges for Premium membership are shown at the time of purchase or in the applicable quotation. SkillBridge does not invent or promise fixed prices in these Terms. Where a Premium price is shown on the Platform, it applies as stated at the time of purchase.

A Premium badge indicates paid membership only. It is not a verification badge and does not confirm the accuracy of any information in a listing.

13. Payments and applicable charges

Charges for paid services, including Premium membership and lead research services, are as shown on the Platform or stated in the applicable quotation or order confirmation.

Where a supplier and a buyer transact directly, payment is made directly between them, unless a separately agreed arrangement states otherwise. SkillBridge is not responsible for payments made directly between users.

Any referral commission must be separately agreed in writing before it applies. No referral commission applies under these Terms.

14. Cancellation and refund references

Cancellation and refund rights for paid services are set out in the Refund and Cancellation Policy (SB-RC-05). That policy applies alongside these Terms. Nothing in these Terms removes any right that you have under applicable law.

15. Intellectual property and content rights

The Platform, its design, text, branding and software are owned by SkillBridge or its licensors. The SkillBridge name, logo and branding may not be used without permission.

You keep ownership of the content you submit, such as your business information, logo, images, profile and portfolio. By submitting content, you give SkillBridge a limited, non-exclusive permission to display that content on the Platform and in related SkillBridge materials, for the purpose of operating the Platform. You confirm that you have the rights needed to give that permission.

You may not copy, scrape, resell or reuse content from the Platform, other than for your own lawful use of the Platform.

16. Prohibited activities, fraud and misleading information

You must not use the Platform to:

  • provide false, misleading or fraudulent information, or impersonate another person or business;
  • claim verification, certification, awards, clients or results that do not exist;
  • send spam, unsolicited bulk messages or harassing communications;
  • collect or misuse other users’ personal information;
  • attempt to interfere with the Platform, its security or its availability;
  • use the Platform for any unlawful purpose.

SkillBridge may report suspected fraud or unlawful activity to the relevant authorities where required by law.

17. Suspension and removal of listings

SkillBridge may suspend or remove any listing, profile or account where, in its reasonable opinion, the information is inaccurate, misleading, duplicated, infringing, or in breach of these Terms or the applicable listing policy. Where practical, SkillBridge will tell the affected user and give them an opportunity to correct the issue.

A suspended listing is not shown on the Platform until the issue is resolved. SkillBridge may restore a listing once it is satisfied that the issue has been corrected.

The Platform may contain links to websites, social media pages, messaging services or other third-party services. SkillBridge does not control and is not responsible for those services, their content or their privacy practices. Your use of third-party services is at your own risk and is subject to their own terms.

These Terms are governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising from these Terms or the Platform will first be discussed in good faith with SkillBridge at skillbridge.manavlab@gmail.com.
