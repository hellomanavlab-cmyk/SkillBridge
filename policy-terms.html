1. Introduction and About SkillBridge

SkillBridge is a business research and lead service powered by ManavLab. SkillBridge offers four services: Raw Leads, Verified Research Leads, Business Analysis (free) and Professional Connection (free).

SkillBridge is not described in this document as a registered company, partnership or other legal entity. Pending confirmation

These Terms apply when you use the SkillBridge website or request any service from SkillBridge.

2. Meaning of Terms and Definitions

  • “SkillBridge”, “we”, “us” means the SkillBridge initiative powered by ManavLab.
  • “You” or “Customer” means a person or business that requests or uses a SkillBridge service.
  • “Lead” means a business record. The standard fields are Business Name, Phone Number, Email (if available), Website (if available) and Location.
  • “Raw Leads” means basic lead data as described in Section 5.
  • “Verified Research Leads” means leads that have gone through the additional research and verification described in Section 6 and in SB-PG Section A.
  • “Business Analysis” means the free research report described in Section 7 and in SB-BA.
  • “Professional Connection” means the free introduction service described in Section 8 and in SB-PG Section C.
  • “Order” means a request for a paid or free service that SkillBridge has received and confirmed.
  • “Website” means the SkillBridge website at https://hellomanavlab-cmyk.github.io/SkillBridge/.

5. Raw Leads Service Terms

  1. Standard fields. Each Raw Lead contains, where available: Business Name, Phone Number, Email (if available), Website (if available) and Business Location.
  2. No guaranteed completeness. Not every Raw Lead will contain every field. Some leads may contain fewer fields.
  3. Free limit. The first 10 Raw Leads are free, one time per customer or business. The free limit does not restart with each new order.
  4. Paid Raw Leads. After the free limit, Raw Leads cost ₹99 per 10 Raw Leads.
  5. Payment before delivery. Paid Raw Leads are transferred only after full payment has been completed. Free Raw Leads under the one-time limit are transferred without payment.
  6. No guarantee. Raw Leads are not guaranteed to be accurate, current, responsive or interested. See Section 11.

6. Verified Research Leads Service Terms

  1. Price. ₹799 per 10 Verified Research Leads.
  2. Additional research. Verified Research Leads go through an additional research and verification process of the available information. The research activities are described in SB-PG Section A. Specific verification steps are being confirmed. Pending confirmation
  3. Meaning of verified. “Verified” means that the listed information has been researched and checked against the available sources described in SB-PG Section A. It does not mean that the contact is interested, ready to buy, or will respond. It also does not mean that every field is complete.
  4. Payment before delivery. Verified Research Leads are transferred only after full payment has been completed.
  5. No guarantee. See Section 11.

7. Business Analysis Service Terms

  1. Free service. Business Analysis is completely free. No paid package is offered.
  2. Purpose. The analysis helps understand a business, identify problems and suggest practical improvement opportunities.
  3. Information you provide. You provide the business name, location, website and social links, products or services, main problems, requirements and contact details.
  4. Report. The report follows the eight-section structure described in SB-PG Section B.
  5. Basis of the analysis. The analysis is based on the information you provide and on publicly available sources.
  6. Not a guarantee. The analysis and recommendations are not a guarantee of business results. They are not legal, tax or financial advice.

8. Professional Connection Service Terms

  1. Free service. Professional Connection is completely free for business owners and for professionals.
  2. Zero fees. There is no introduction fee.
  3. Zero commission. SkillBridge takes no commission. This includes no percentage of project value and no commission after project completion.
  4. Introductions with permission. SkillBridge introduces a business owner and a professional only with the permission of both parties.
  5. Own agreement. The business owner and the professional are responsible for their own agreement, payments and project execution. SkillBridge is not a party to that agreement.
  6. No verification claim. Professional skills are not verified unless a verification system is implemented and described on the website.
  7. No guarantee. See Section 11.

9. Pricing and Payment Conditions

  1. Prices. The prices are:
    • Raw Leads: the first 10 Raw Leads are free, one time per customer or business. After that, ₹99 per 10 Raw Leads.
    • Verified Research Leads: ₹799 per 10 leads.
    • Business Analysis: free.
    • Professional Connection: free, with zero introduction fee and zero commission.
  2. Price applicable. The price is the price shown on the Website when the order is confirmed.
  3. Payment before delivery. For both paid lead services, full payment must be completed before the paid leads are transferred to you. This applies to paid Raw Leads (after the free limit) and to all Verified Research Leads.
  4. Free Raw Leads. The first 10 Raw Leads under the one-time free limit are transferred without payment.
  5. Payment method. The payment method and payment processor are not described in this draft. Pending confirmation
  6. Taxes. Pending confirmation Applicable taxes, if any, will be stated before payment. Taxes are not described in this draft until confirmed.

10. Lead Delivery and Transfer Conditions

  1. Delivery method. Leads and reports are delivered by the method agreed at the time of order, such as WhatsApp or email.
  2. Transfer after payment. For paid services, transfer happens after full payment is complete (see Section 9.3).
  3. Delivery timelines. Any delivery timeline is a target and not a guarantee. Pending confirmation
  4. Partial delivery. If a delivery is partial, SkillBridge will tell you what has been delivered and what remains. See SB-PG Section D.
  5. Format. Leads may be shared as a list or file in the format agreed at order.

11. No Guarantee and Limitation of Expectations

This Section applies to both Raw Leads and Verified Research Leads.

SkillBridge does not guarantee any of the following:

  • Customer responses;
  • Buyer interest;
  • Meetings;
  • Sales;
  • Conversions;
  • Revenue;
  • Business growth;
  • Successful projects.

Verified Research Leads mean additional research and verification of available information. They do not mean guaranteed buyer intent.

13. Business Owner Responsibilities

If you are a business owner, you must:

  1. Provide accurate information for Business Analysis and for any Professional Connection request;
  2. Give permission before any introduction is made to a professional;
  3. Make your own decisions about whether to act on any recommendation;
  4. Enter into your own agreement with any professional you choose.

14. Professional Responsibilities

  1. Describe your skills and experience accurately;
  2. Give permission before your profile is shared with a business owner;
  3. Carry out any project under your own agreement with the business owner;
  4. Comply with applicable laws in your work.
