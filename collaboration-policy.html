Business Collaboration Policy

1. What collaboration means here

Business collaboration is a research and introduction service. SkillBridge helps businesses and independent professionals explore potentially relevant connections, such as suppliers and buyers, retailers and manufacturers, distributors, or complementary service partners.

2. What we do

  • Review the requirements you submit.
  • Research potentially relevant businesses using available information.
  • Assess suitability and explain the reasons for any suggestion.
  • Arrange an introduction only where it is suitable and permitted, and only with the consent of the people involved.

3. What we do not guarantee

SkillBridge does not guarantee a partnership, supplier relationship, buyer, customer, contract, sale, or revenue. Every connection depends on both parties' decisions, suitability, and market conditions.

4. Sharing contact details

We do not share one party's private contact information with another without an appropriate legal basis and that party's permission.

5. Separate from lead generation

Business collaboration is a different service from lead generation. Lead generation is governed by the Lead and Opportunity Policy. Collaboration is governed by this policy, the Collaboration Terms, and the Collaboration Refund Policy.

6. Fees

Free registration and submitting a request do not require payment. Any fee, commission, or other commercial term for a specific collaboration is set out in writing before it applies. SkillBridge does not publish a standard commission rate for collaborations.

Information the owner still needs to provide

  • Registered legal name and business status of the entity that operates SkillBridge (for example sole proprietorship or company)
  • Registered business address, if it is to be published
  • Governing law and courts with jurisdiction for disputes
  • Name and contact of the person handling privacy and grievance requests (and the response time you commit to)
  • Whether any collaboration fee or commission will ever apply, and if so, the written terms that will govern it
  • Response time you commit to for collaboration requests
