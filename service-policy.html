Scope

Each service is assessed individually. We confirm scope, deliverables, timeline, budget and responsibilities before starting.

Research and analysis

Reports distinguish verified observations, evidence and sources, potential problems, possible causes, recommendations, priorities and information that remains unverified. We do not state that a problem exists unless evidence supports it.

Client responsibilities

Provide accurate information, timely feedback and any access needed for the agreed work.

Delivery and review

Deliverables are provided as agreed. Reasonable review and correction rounds are defined in the project agreement.

Availability

Services may be declined or paused if we cannot deliver them properly or if a request is unlawful or unsuitable.

No guarantees

Services support decision-making. They do not guarantee customers, sales, revenue, rankings or growth.
