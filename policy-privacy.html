1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SkillBridge (powered by ManavLab) collects, uses, shares, stores and protects personal information when you use the SkillBridge platform, including the website and related pages (the “Platform”). It should be read together with the Terms and Conditions (SB-TC-01).

This Policy describes both information that is available on the Platform today and information that may be used in future as SkillBridge develops. Where a feature is planned rather than live, this is stated clearly in Section 12.

2. Information SkillBridge collects

Depending on how you use the Platform, SkillBridge may collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Client information

  • name;
  • email address;
  • phone number or WhatsApp number;
  • location;
  • requirements, enquiry details and description of what you are looking for;
  • optional budget or quantity, if you choose to provide it;
  • your consent to be connected with a relevant business or freelancer, where applicable.

2.2 Business owner information

  • business name;
  • name and role of the authorised representative;
  • business contact details, including phone number and email address;
  • location;
  • business category;
  • products and services;
  • website and social media links;
  • logo, images and other business content you submit;
  • your publication permissions (see Section 6).

2.3 Freelancer information

  • name;
  • contact details;
  • location;
  • skills and services;
  • portfolio and work samples;
  • experience details;
  • your publication permissions (see Section 6).

2.4 Lead research information

  • business names;
  • publicly available business contact information;
  • industry and location;
  • websites and public profiles;
  • source references and research dates;
  • research status, such as Raw, Under Review or Report Delivered.

3. Why information is collected

SkillBridge collects information only for the purposes described in this Policy. These include:

  • reviewing and approving listings and profiles;
  • responding to client enquiries and connecting clients with relevant businesses or freelancers, where consent has been given;
  • preparing and delivering research reports and lead research services;
  • communicating with you about your listing, enquiry, order or account;
  • maintaining the security and integrity of the Platform;
  • meeting legal and record-keeping obligations where applicable.

4. How information is used

SkillBridge uses information to operate the Platform, review submissions, handle enquiries, prepare research, communicate with users and improve the Platform. Information is not used for purposes that are incompatible with the purposes above without your consent or as required by law.

SkillBridge does not sell personal information.

5. Public and private information

Some information may be shown publicly on the Platform once a listing or profile is published, with your permission. Other information remains private.

6. Publication consent

6.1 Business owners

A business owner may give permission for SkillBridge to publish the business profile, logo and images. Permission to publish a public phone number or email address is given separately. Giving permission for one does not mean permission for the other.

6.2 Freelancers

A freelancer may give permission for SkillBridge to publish profile information, skills, services and portfolio items. Permission to publish a public phone number or email address is given separately.

You may withdraw publication permission at any time by contacting SkillBridge. SkillBridge will then remove or stop showing the relevant information in line with Section 10.

7. Client enquiries

When you submit an enquiry, SkillBridge uses the information to review your requirement, check the contact details you have provided and, where you have given consent, consider a suitable business or freelancer to connect you with.

Your enquiry is not passed to a business or freelancer for the purpose of contacting you until you have given consent for that connection. You may withdraw consent for a connection at any time.

8. Lead research information and sources

SkillBridge may research businesses using publicly available information. This may include business names, publicly available business contact information, industry, location, websites and public profiles.

Each research record keeps a reference to the source where it was found and the date of research, so that the information can be checked. Research information is used only for the research service and related communication. SkillBridge distinguishes between confirmed facts and research-based observations.

Publicly available business contact information used in research is not automatically treated as consent for that business to be contacted by any client or provider. Contact must follow the consent and contact-sharing rules in Section 9.

9. Sharing information with businesses or freelancers

SkillBridge will not share private contact details with another business or freelancer without the permission of the person concerned. Before sharing private contact details, SkillBridge requires that:

  • the person has given clear consent for that specific connection;
  • the purpose of the connection is clear to all parties;
  • the person has been told which information will be shared.

Public information that has been published with permission may be seen by other users of the Platform. SkillBridge is not responsible for how other users use information that is publicly available.

10. Data storage, security and retention

SkillBridge handles personal information manually at this stage, for the purposes described in this Policy. Access is limited to the people at SkillBridge who need the information to review submissions, handle enquiries, prepare research and communicate with users.

SkillBridge takes reasonable care to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. SkillBridge does not currently state that it operates a specific database, encrypted storage system, security certification or secure hosting environment. If any such system is introduced, this Policy will be updated to describe it before it is relied upon.

Personal information is kept only for as long as it is needed for the purposes in this Policy, or as required by law. Where information is no longer needed, SkillBridge will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

11. Correction and deletion requests

You may ask SkillBridge to correct or delete your personal information, or to stop showing a listing or profile. To make a request, contact skillbridge.manavlab@gmail.com with enough detail for SkillBridge to identify your listing, enquiry or record.

SkillBridge will review each request and respond within a reasonable time. Some information may need to be kept where required by law or for legitimate record-keeping, in which case SkillBridge will tell you.

12. Third-party tools; current and planned features

12.1 Third-party tools

SkillBridge may use third-party tools for email, document preparation, scheduling or other operational purposes. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. SkillBridge will tell users about any third-party service that is materially relevant to how their information is handled.

12.2 Current and planned features

The following statements apply to this Policy as drafted:

  • SkillBridge does not currently state that it operates a user login system, customer accounts, cookies, analytics or tracking, automated data collection or an online payment gateway.
  • Where any of these features are introduced, this Policy will be updated before the feature is used, and the updated Policy will describe what information is involved.
  • Planned features are not live until SkillBridge confirms they are available on the Platform.
