1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information SkillBridge handles, why we handle it, and what choices you have. It separates three things: what the Website verifiably does today, what you choose to include in a message, and what may be held by third parties or in historical records.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only through the forms and contact details you choose to use:

Source Fields
Raw Leads form Name, email, category, location, lead count, additional requirements
Verified Research Leads form Name, email, category, location, requirements, additional details
Contact form Name, email, WhatsApp number, topic, message
Business Analysis Business name, location, website and social links, products or services, main problems, requirements, contact details (form not yet published) Pending confirmation
Professional Connection Details you provide for a requested introduction (form not yet published) Pending confirmation

3. How Forms Work (verified in website code)

  1. When you fill in a form and press the WhatsApp or email button, the form prepares a message containing the information you entered.
  2. The message opens in WhatsApp or in your email application.
  3. You must press Send in that external application. The message is not delivered until you do so.
  4. The reviewed forms do not send your information automatically to a SkillBridge server or database.

4. Business Information and Personal Information

  • Business information includes business names, public business phone numbers, business addresses, business websites and public business social pages.
  • Personal information includes an individual’s name, personal email address, personal phone number and any other information that identifies a person.
  • Business information being publicly visible does not mean that the person has agreed to receive marketing messages. We do not treat public visibility as consent for marketing.

5. Contact Information

When you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email, WhatsApp number, topic and message. We use it to reply to you.

6. Information Provided Through Enquiry Forms

Enquiry information is used to:

  1. Respond to your enquiry;
  2. Prepare the lead list, report or introduction you requested;
  3. Communicate with you about your order.

7. Information Collected During Business Analysis

For Business Analysis, we use the information you provide and publicly available information from the sources listed in SB-BA. We do not collect information from private accounts or private areas of any platform.

8. Information Used for Lead Research

Lead research uses publicly available business information from the sources described in SB-PG Section A, and the information you supply. Lead records contain business details (Business Name, Phone Number, Email if available, Website if available, Location).

9. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  1. Respond to enquiries and requests;
  2. Prepare requested leads, reports and introductions;
  3. Deliver paid and free services;
  4. Handle refunds, corrections and complaints;
  5. Meet legal obligations.

10. How We Store and Handle Information

  1. Information in forms stays in your device until you press Send in WhatsApp or email.
  2. After sending, the message is held by WhatsApp or by your email provider, and by the email account that receives it. Their handling is governed by their own policies.
  3. Where SkillBridge keeps records of enquiries, orders or lead lists, the storage location and access arrangements are Pending confirmation and Pending confirmation.

11. Sharing of Information

  1. We do not sell personal information.
  2. We share information only where needed to provide the service you requested, or where the law requires it.
  3. Service providers used for WhatsApp, email and website hosting receive information as needed for those services. Pending confirmation

12. Sharing Information With Business Owners

Information about a professional is shared with a business owner only for an introduction that both parties have agreed to. Information about a business owner is shared with a professional only for the same agreed introduction.

13. Sharing Information With Professionals

See Section 12. No information is shared with a professional without permission from the relevant person.

14. Third-Party Websites and Services

  1. WhatsApp and email. Messages you send are handled by WhatsApp or your email provider under their own policies.
  2. Google Fonts. The Website loads fonts from Google. When you visit the Website, Google may receive your IP address for this purpose.
  3. External links. The Website may link to Google, social media platforms and other external websites. Their privacy practices apply when you visit them.

15. Cookies and Browser Storage

Verified in the website code, the Website stores two items in your browser’s local storage:

Key Purpose
sb_cookie_consent Records your cookie consent choice (Accept or Reject).
sb_diwali_seen Records that a seasonal banner has been shown in your browser. Pending confirmation
  1. These items stay in your browser. They are not sent to SkillBridge automatically.
  2. No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts were found in the reviewed website code.
  3. You can clear browser storage at any time through your browser settings.
  4. The Cookie Policy describes these items in more detail.
