A. Lead Generation Service Policy

A.1 Overview

SkillBridge provides two lead services:

  1. Raw Leads: basic lead data.
  2. Verified Research Leads: leads that have gone through additional research and verification of the available information.

A.2 Raw Leads

  1. Fields. Business Name, Phone Number, Email (if available), Website (if available), Business Location.
  2. Not every field in every lead. Some leads will not contain every field.
  3. Free limit. The first 10 Raw Leads are free, one time per customer or business. The free limit does not restart with each new order.
  4. Price after the free limit. ₹99 per 10 Raw Leads.
  5. Full payment before transfer. Paid Raw Leads are transferred only after full payment is complete.

A.3 Verified Research Leads

  1. Price. ₹799 per 10 Verified Research Leads.
  2. Additional research and verification. Verified Research Leads have gone through additional research and verification of the available information. The specific activities are listed below in A.4.
  3. Meaning of “verified”. “Verified” means that the listed information has been researched and checked against the sources in A.4. It does not mean that the contact is interested, ready to buy, or will respond. It does not mean that every field is complete.
  4. Full payment before transfer. Verified Research Leads are transferred only after full payment is complete.

A.4 Research Methodology

  1. Confirmed research activity. Both lead services involve research of information available from public sources, and from information supplied by the business where relevant.
  2. Sources. Google Search, Google Maps, business websites, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. Pending confirmation
  3. Specific verification steps for Verified Research Leads. The specific checks performed for each field are: Pending confirmation. Until these steps are listed, Verified Research Leads are described only as “additional research and verification of available information.”

A.5 Delivery

  1. Leads are delivered by the method agreed at order (WhatsApp or email).
  2. Paid leads are delivered after full payment.
  3. Delivery timelines are targets. Pending confirmation

A.6 Pricing Summary

Service Price
Raw Leads First 10 free (one time per customer/business). Then ₹99 per 10.
Verified Research Leads ₹799 per 10 leads

A.7 Lead Limitations

  1. Lead information may be incomplete, outdated or incorrect.
  2. Information comes from third-party and public sources that SkillBridge does not control.
  3. Duplicate or incorrect records may be reported for correction under Section D.

A.8 No Guarantee Policy (applies to both lead services)

SkillBridge does not guarantee any of the following for either Raw Leads or Verified Research Leads:

  • Customer responses;
  • Buyer interest;
  • Meetings;
  • Sales;
  • Conversions;
  • Revenue;
  • Business growth;
  • Successful projects.

B. Business Analysis Policy

B.1 Purpose

Business Analysis helps understand a business, identify problems and suggest practical improvement opportunities.

B.2 Free Service

  1. Business Analysis is completely free.
  2. No paid package is offered for Business Analysis.

B.3 Information Required From the Business Owner

  • Business name
  • Business location
  • Website and social media links
  • Products or services
  • Main business problems
  • Requirements
  • Contact details

B.4 Research Process

  1. The business owner submits the information in B.3.
  2. SkillBridge researches the business using the sources in B.5.
  3. SkillBridge reviews the findings and prepares a report.
  4. The report is shared with the business owner by the method agreed (WhatsApp or email).

B.5 Research Sources

  • Google Search
  • Google Maps
  • Business website
  • Instagram
  • Facebook
  • LinkedIn
  • Information directly supplied by the business owner

B.6 Analysis Methodology

The analysis compares the information from the sources in B.5 against the business owner’s stated products or services and problems. The findings are recorded with their sources in Section 4 of the report.

B.7 Report Structure

Each Business Analysis report has eight sections:

  1. Business Overview
  2. Online Presence Analysis
  3. Identified Problems
  4. Evidence and Research Sources
  5. Improvement Opportunities
  6. Recommended Solutions
  7. Suitable Professional Requirement, if needed
  8. Final Summary

B.8 Recommendations

  1. Recommendations are based on the information available at the time of the analysis.
  2. Recommendations are suggestions only. The business owner decides whether to act on them.
  3. Where a recommendation needs a professional, Section 7 of the report says so.

B.9 Limitations

  1. The analysis is based on available information and may be incomplete.
  2. The analysis is not a guarantee of business results.
  3. The analysis is not legal, tax or financial advice.

C. Professional Connection Policy

C.1 Purpose

Professional Connection introduces business owners to suitable professionals and freelancers, with permission from both sides.

C.2 Free Service and Zero Commission

  1. Professional Connection is completely free for business owners and for professionals.
  2. There is no introduction fee.
  3. SkillBridge takes no commission. This includes no percentage of project value and no commission after project completion.

C.3 Business Owner Process

  1. The business owner submits a requirement.
  2. SkillBridge identifies a possibly suitable professional.
  3. SkillBridge asks the business owner for permission before any introduction.
  4. If the business owner agrees, the introduction is made.

C.4 Professional Process

  1. The professional shares a profile or details of skills.
  2. SkillBridge asks the professional for permission before sharing the profile with a business owner.
  3. If the professional agrees, the profile is shared as part of an agreed introduction.

C.5 Introduction and Permission

  1. No introduction is made without permission from both the business owner and the professional.
  2. Permission may be withdrawn before the introduction is made.

C.6 Sharing Professional Profiles

  1. Profiles are shared only as far as the professional has agreed.
  2. Skills are described as the professional provides them.

C.7 Responsibilities of Both Parties

  • The business owner and the professional are each responsible for their own agreement, payments and project execution.
  • SkillBridge is not a party to any project agreement.

C.8 Project Execution Responsibility

Project scope, timelines, quality and payments are agreed directly between the business owner and the professional. SkillBridge is not responsible for project execution.

C.9 Skills

Professional skills are not verified unless a verification system is implemented and described on the Website. Pending confirmation

C.10 No Guarantee

SkillBridge does not guarantee any project, work, earnings or outcome from an introduction.

F. Lead Usage & Responsible Communication Guidelines

  1. Lawful use. Use lead information only for lawful, legitimate business purposes.
  2. No spam. Do not send unsolicited, repetitive or unwanted messages.
  3. No misleading communication. Do not make false or misleading claims about your business, your offer or the lead.
  4. Respect objections. Respect any objection or opt-out request immediately, and remove the person from your list when asked.
  5. No misuse or resale. Do not resell, share publicly or misuse lead data.
  6. Applicable requirements. Follow applicable privacy and communication laws, including:
    • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules;
    • The Information Technology Act, 2000 for electronic communication;
    • Any messaging platform rules, such as WhatsApp’s business messaging policies.
  7. Legal review. These guidelines must be reviewed by a qualified Indian lawyer. Pending confirmation

G. General Service Guidelines

  1. Acceptable use. Use the Website and services lawfully, accurately and without abuse.
  2. User responsibilities. Provide accurate information, make payments on time where required, and use outputs lawfully.
  3. Accuracy limitations. Information depends on public and provided sources and may be incomplete or outdated.
  4. External platform limitations. Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and other platforms have their own terms, which apply to your use of them.
  5. Policy violations. A violation of these policies may lead to refusal or suspension of services under SB-TC Section 19.
  6. Updates. Policy changes are published on the Website with a version number and date.
