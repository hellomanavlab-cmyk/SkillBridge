About SkillBridge

SkillBridge is a lead generation and business research service. It is powered by ManavLab.

Why it exists

Finding the right business partner, supplier or client is still hard for many small businesses and independent professionals. SkillBridge makes that first step clearer, with research first and transparency about what is verified.

How Raw Leads work

  1. You send a request with your category, location and number of leads.
  2. We research relevant businesses manually.
  3. The leads are delivered privately to you.

The first 10 leads are free. Additional 10 leads are ₹99.

How Verified Leads work

  1. You send an enquiry with your category, location and requirements.
  2. We research selected businesses manually and check the sources.
  3. You receive an individual PDF report for each researched business.

Reports describe what we found from public sources. They do not mean a business wants to buy a service.

How SkillBridge helps freelancers and businesses

Freelancers, agencies and businesses get researched information about relevant businesses, so their first conversations start with context. SkillBridge does not guarantee leads, replies, meetings or sales.
