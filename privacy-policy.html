Who we are

SkillBridge is a project powered by ManavLab. This policy explains how information is handled when you use this website, submit an inquiry or use a SkillBridge account. Contact: hello.manavlab@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Inquiry form (Google Forms): name, business email, business or organisation name, website or social link, and the details you choose to write about your business needs. Google Forms may also record the email address of the respondent.
  • SkillBridge account (if you have one): name, email, and profile details you enter such as phone, country, city, skills, services, portfolio link, target industries and markets; plus campaign, opportunity and plan data you create or that is created for your account.
  • Messages you send us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Technical storage in your browser, described in the Cookie Policy.

How we use it

  • To respond to inquiries and assess whether our services may be relevant.
  • To deliver services you have agreed to and to operate your account.
  • To keep the service secure and to prevent misuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers to run the site and handle data, including Google (Forms), Supabase (account database and authentication) and the website hosting provider. Specialists or other third parties receive your information only where it is needed for a project you have approved and, where appropriate, with your permission.

Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. You may ask us to correct or delete your information by emailing hello.manavlab@gmail.com. Specific retention periods are to be confirmed (see the box below).

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information and can withdraw consent for optional processing at any time. Indian data-protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, may apply to this processing; the exact rights, timelines and notices should be confirmed with a qualified legal professional.

Security

We use access controls on accounts and database records so users can only see their own information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and professionals and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
