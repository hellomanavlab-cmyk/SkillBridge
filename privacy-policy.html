Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · SkillBridge, powered by ManavLab

Draft — pending legal review. This page is a working draft dated 23 October 2026 and has not been reviewed by a qualified legal professional. Items in square brackets must be confirmed by the owner before publication.

1. Who we are

SkillBridge is a project powered by ManavLab. This website is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. For any privacy question or request, write to skillbridge.manavlab@gmail.com. You may also use the additional contact hello.manavlab@gmail.com.

2. What this website does and does not do

  • It does not have a public account system. You cannot create a profile or log in on this website.
  • It does not have a public database. Form details are not stored on our server.
  • It does not take payments. There is no online payment gateway. Payment details are shared privately only after you agree the terms with us.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Forms open a WhatsApp or email message draft on your device. You decide whether to send it.

3. Information you choose to send

When you use a form and send the message, the details you typed go to the WhatsApp or email draft you send. These can include: your name, email, WhatsApp number, business or professional details, location, industry, requirements, and any notes you add. Please send only the information you need for your request.

4. Why we use it

We use the information you send only to respond to your request, understand your requirement, prepare a quote or research, and communicate with you about it.

5. Browser storage on this site

  • Cookie choice: your Accept or Reject choice is saved in your browser so the banner does not appear again.
  • Session flags: the introduction and the Diwali greeting use one flag each in your browser session, so they do not repeat during the same visit. These flags are stored only in your browser and are not sent to us.

6. Who receives your information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information reaches these parties only in these cases:

  • GitHub, which hosts this static website and delivers the pages to your browser. GitHub may keep standard server logs, which can include your IP address.
  • Google Fonts, which the website loads for typefaces. Your browser contacts Google when the page loads, and Google may receive your IP address.
  • Google, if you choose to use the Google Form for registration. Google handles the form under its own terms.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to send a message through WhatsApp.
  • Your email provider, and ours, if you choose to send an email.
  • A specialist or partner, only for a project or introduction you have approved, and only with your permission.
  • Public authorities, only if the law requires it.

7. Business research and public information

Where SkillBridge researches businesses, it uses publicly available information. We do not collect private personal data for this purpose. Information about a business may be incomplete or out of date, and we tell you where information could not be verified.

8. Payments

We do not store card numbers, bank passwords or one-time codes. Never send payment details or one-time codes to anyone claiming to be SkillBridge without first checking with us on the contact details above.

9. Security

This is a static website, and it does not store form details on a server. Messages you send travel through WhatsApp or your email provider, which have their own security. No internet transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a personal data breach, we will act as the law requires.

10. How long we keep information

Information you send by WhatsApp or email is kept by the relevant messaging provider and in our own messages and records for as long as needed to respond to your request and to meet legal and accounting obligations. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: retention period for your WhatsApp and email records, and how requests for deletion are handled.]

You can ask us to delete the records we hold about you. We will do so unless we must keep them by law.

11. Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, and withdraw consent for a request you made. Write to skillbridge.manavlab@gmail.com. Indian data-protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, may apply. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: response time for privacy requests. Legal review required before publication.]

12. Cookies, children and changes

We use only the browser storage described in section 5, and no advertising or analytics cookies. See the Cookie Policy. This website is not directed at children under 18. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
