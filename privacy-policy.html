Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · SkillBridge, powered by ManavLab

Draft — pending legal review. This page is a working draft dated 23 October 2026 and has not been reviewed by a qualified legal professional. Items in square brackets must be confirmed by the owner before publication.

1. Who we are

SkillBridge is a project powered by ManavLab. For every privacy question or request, write to hello.manavlab@gmail.com.

2. Our privacy commitments

  • We never sell or rent customer personal information.
  • We do not share customer information with unrelated websites, companies, data brokers or other third parties for their own use.
  • We do not submit customer personal data to AI tools for model training.
  • We do not disclose one customer's order, requirements, contact details or business information to another customer.
  • We collect and use only the information needed to provide the service you asked for.
  • Where we use a service provider to run SkillBridge, we name it in this policy and explain what it processes.

3. Why we collect your information

We collect information only to:

  • reply to your inquiry and understand what you need;
  • check whether our lead generation, collaboration or expert services suit you;
  • prepare your order, quote and invoice, and confirm your payment;
  • deliver the research or service you ordered;
  • contact you about your own request;
  • keep the website and accounts secure, and meet legal and accounting obligations.

4. What we collect and how it moves

  • Inquiries and order details you choose to give: name, business name, email, WhatsApp or phone number, website or social link, country, city, target industry or audience, number of leads, service, and notes.
  • Collaboration requests and expert applications: the details you enter on those forms.
  • Account information, only if you create or sign in to a SkillBridge account: name, email and profile details.
  • Basic technical data sent automatically when you visit any website, such as your IP address and browser type.

The order and lead forms on this website do not send your details to our server automatically. Your details leave your device only when you press Send in WhatsApp or your email app, or when you submit a Google Form.

5. Service providers that process your data

These providers are technically necessary to run SkillBridge. Each processes data under its own terms and privacy policy, and we name them so you know where your data may go:

  • Google (Google Forms): stores the responses you submit to our inquiry, collaboration and expert forms.
  • Google (Google Fonts): when a page loads its fonts, your browser contacts Google, which can receive your IP address.
  • Google (Gmail, for hello.manavlab@gmail.com): stores emails you send to us.
  • Meta (WhatsApp): carries the messages you send to us when you press Send in WhatsApp.
  • Supabase: hosts the SkillBridge database and sign-in for accounts. It holds account and dashboard records only for people who create or sign in to an account.
  • Website hosting provider [Vercel, to be confirmed by the owner]: serves the website and keeps standard server logs, which can include your IP address.
  • Automation tools (not active yet): SkillBridge's planned research and reporting workflows may use a research data service, an AI language model and an email-sending service. They will be used only after the owner has configured and reviewed them, and this policy will be updated before they handle any customer data.

6. AI tools and model training

We do not enter your personal data into AI tools to train their models, and we do not enter confidential customer information into any AI tool for that purpose. If an AI service is ever used to process your information, we will: use only a service whose terms and settings say it does not use customer inputs for training; send only the minimum fields needed for the task; and update this policy before doing so.

7. Sale, sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only in these cases:

  • with the service providers listed in section 5, only as needed to run SkillBridge;
  • with a specialist or partner, only for a project or introduction you have approved and only with your permission;
  • with public authorities, only when the law requires it.

8. Lead research and customer separation

Prospect information in our research comes from publicly available sources. Each customer's requirements, orders and contact details are kept separate, and we never disclose one customer's private information to another. We do not sell prospect lists.

9. Payments

We do not store card numbers, bank passwords or one-time codes on this website. Payment details are shared privately by us only after your order is confirmed. Never send payment details or one-time codes to anyone claiming to be SkillBridge without first checking with us on the contact details above.

10. Security

We protect data with access controls. Database tables enforce row-level security, so a signed-in user can read only their own records. Administrative access is restricted to authorised accounts. Secret keys are kept on the server side and are never placed in the website's code. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a personal data breach, we will act as the law requires.

11. How long we keep your information

In simple words: we keep records for as long as they are needed. We do not delete everything after a fixed period, and we do not keep personal information forever for no reason.

  • Active accounts: we keep your account information for as long as your account stays active.
  • Orders, invoices and business records: we keep these for as long as needed for our legitimate business purposes, such as delivering and supporting your order, answering questions about past work, and handling disputes, and for as long as applicable law requires, including accounting and tax records.
  • Inquiries, collaboration requests and expert applications that did not lead to an order or project: we review them periodically and delete or anonymise them when they are no longer needed.
  • Periodic review: we review inactive records and other personal information on a regular schedule [review frequency to be confirmed by the owner].
  • Deletion requests: when you ask us to delete your information, we do so, unless the law requires us to keep certain records (for example, invoices and tax records). In that case we keep only what the law requires and tell you why.

We cannot promise to keep every personal detail forever. Storage must always be balanced against data-protection principles such as purpose limitation and data minimisation, and against applicable law.

12. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, to stop using it for a request you made, and to change your cookie choice at any time from the Cookie preferences link in the footer. For all privacy requests, write to hello.manavlab@gmail.com. Indian data-protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, may apply. The exact rights and response times are [to be confirmed by a qualified legal professional].

13. Cookies, children and changes

We use only essential browser storage, and optional cookies only if you accept them (see the Cookie Policy). This website is not directed at children under 18. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
