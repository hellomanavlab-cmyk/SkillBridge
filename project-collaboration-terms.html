Project Collaboration Terms

Before work starts

A project begins only after scope, deliverables, budget, timeline, responsibilities and payment terms are agreed in writing.

Specialist involvement

Where a specialist is suggested, the client approves the involvement and the terms. Contact details are shared only with permission.

Milestones and delivery

Milestones, review points and delivery are set out in the agreement. Deliverables are reviewed against the agreed scope.

Changes

Changes to scope, cost or timeline are agreed in writing before the extra work is done.

Confidentiality

Information shared for a project is used only for that project and is not disclosed to others except as agreed or required by law.

Ending a project

A project may end on completion, by agreement, or according to the cancellation terms in the agreement and the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
