What the network is

The expert network is a group of independent professionals who may be considered for projects on a project basis.

No guarantee of work

Membership or interest does not guarantee projects, clients, income or assignments. Work depends on real client requirements, budget, availability, approval and agreement.

Applications and review

Applications are reviewed on the information provided, such as skills, portfolio and availability. Approval for the network is separate from approval for any specific project.

Published profiles

Profiles are published only when the information is real and the specialist has given permission. We do not publish invented experience, qualifications, ratings or client names.

Independent professionals

Specialists work independently and are responsible for their own tools, taxes and compliance, unless an agreement states otherwise.

Conduct

Specialists must give accurate information, respect client confidentiality, and deliver work they are able to complete.
