FAQ

Common questions.

Are SkillBridge opportunities guaranteed clients?

No. SkillBridge provides researched potential opportunities, not guaranteed customers.

What do I receive?

A campaign-specific spreadsheet containing researched opportunity information and relevant context.

Can SkillBridge contact prospects for me?

The Outreach Agent can handle approved outreach through supported and authorized channels, within defined limits.

Can I use my own email?

Where supported, yes — through secure authorization. SkillBridge never asks for your password.

Does SkillBridge guarantee meetings?

No. Response and meeting rates depend on your offer, market and outreach content.

Can I pause outreach?

Yes. Campaign controls support pausing and stopping outreach at any time.

What happens if fewer opportunities qualify than requested?

SkillBridge prioritizes quality rather than fabricating entries to hit a number.

Is Meta Ads spend included in the SkillBridge Growth price?

Yes — for the Growth plan specifically. Its $180 (≈ ₹15,000)/month fee is all-inclusive: roughly $12/₹1,000 for 100 researched opportunities, $48/₹4,000 for the Outreach Agent, and $108/₹9,000 for the recommended Meta Ads spend. If you buy Meta Ads as a standalone service instead, ad spend is billed separately from the management fee.

How much would standalone Meta Ads cost?

The recommended starting ad spend is ₹300/day, but the actual budget can be adjusted according to campaign requirements and strategy.

Why are prices shown in dollars?

USD figures are shown for reference only. All billing happens in Indian Rupees (INR) at the amount stated alongside it.

Does the Growth Plan guarantee clients?

No. SkillBridge provides researched potential opportunities, outreach automation and advertising services. We do not guarantee clients, responses, meetings, projects, sales, revenue or conversions.

What are the 100 opportunities?

They are researched potential business opportunities based on your selected service, target market and campaign requirements. They are not guaranteed active buyers.

Can I buy only Meta Ads?

Yes. You can enquire about Meta Ads as a standalone service.

Can I combine different services?

Yes. The Flexible option allows you to request a combination of opportunity research, Outreach Agent and Meta Ads.

Can I increase my advertising budget later?

Yes. The advertising budget can be adjusted based on campaign requirements and strategy.
