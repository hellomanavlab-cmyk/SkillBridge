Lead Generation

Turn Business Research Into Potential Opportunities.

SkillBridge researches potential business prospects based on your services, target audience, industry, and preferred market. Each opportunity is intended to help you understand which businesses may be relevant to your offer and why.

What you can receive

  • Business name and website, where available
  • Business location and industry
  • Relevant publicly available contact information, where available
  • Potential business need identified through research
  • Suggested service offering
  • Explanation of why the business may be relevant
  • Research source or supporting evidence, where available
  • Suggested outreach approach
  • Organized lead spreadsheet

Research findings depend on the information available and on your requirements. Opportunities are potential prospects, not confirmed buyers, and do not guarantee a reply, meeting, or sale.

Business Collaboration

Let's Build Valuable Business Connections.

SkillBridge helps businesses and independent professionals explore potential collaboration opportunities through targeted research, relevant introductions, and structured communication.

Collaboration may include

  • Freelancer and agency partnerships
  • Business-to-business supplier and buyer connections
  • Service-provider partnerships
  • Potential client introductions
  • Project-based collaborations
  • Business development opportunities

How collaboration works

  1. Step 1 — Tell Us About Your Business

    You explain your business, products or services, target audience, and collaboration requirements.

  2. Step 2 — Understand the Requirement

    SkillBridge reviews the submitted information and identifies the type of opportunity being sought.

  3. Step 3 — Explore Potential Matches

    We research potential businesses, partners, suppliers, or buyers based on the requirements and available information.

  4. Step 4 — Review and Connect

    Suitable opportunities may be presented for review, with introductions handled appropriately and with consent where required.

  5. Step 5 — Discuss the Opportunity

    The relevant parties discuss their requirements, commercial terms, and next steps.

We do not guarantee that a collaboration, sale, contract, or partnership will result.
