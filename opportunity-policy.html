Potential Customer Research and Opportunity Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · SkillBridge, powered by ManavLab

Draft — pending legal review. This page is a working draft prepared on 17 October 2026 and has not been reviewed by a qualified legal professional. Items still to be confirmed by the owner are listed at the end.

What an opportunity is

A researched opportunity is a business or prospect that may be relevant to a client's service or target market, based on publicly available information and analysis.

What it is not

  • Not a verified buyer or active customer.
  • Not a promise that the business will reply, meet, buy or become a client.
  • Not a guarantee that contact details are current or complete.

Scores and priority

Opportunity scores and priorities are prioritisation indicators based on research. They are not a probability of conversion.

Sources and accuracy

We use publicly available and permitted sources. If an opportunity cannot be sufficiently researched we leave it out rather than invent details. Information can change; clients should verify before relying on it.

Outreach

Outreach is only carried out through supported, authorised channels and with the client's approval. Recipients who opt out or bounce are not contacted again by automated follow-ups. SkillBridge never asks clients for email passwords.

Client responsibility

Clients remain responsible for the content of their outreach and for complying with laws and platform rules that apply to their communications.

  • Registered business address (only if you want it published)
  • Governing law and jurisdiction for disputes
  • Whether you will remove a researched prospect on request, and how requests are handled
