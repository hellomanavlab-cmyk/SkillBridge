Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · SkillBridge, powered by ManavLab

Draft — pending legal review. This page is a working draft prepared on 17 October 2026 and has not been reviewed by a qualified legal professional. Items still to be confirmed by the owner are listed at the end.

General principle

Cancellation and refund terms for any paid work are set out in the written proposal or agreement for that engagement and are agreed before payment is due.

Before an agreement

Submitting an inquiry is free and creates no payment obligation.

Cancellation

Either party may request cancellation of a project by written notice. Work already completed, third-party costs already incurred and any agreed non-refundable amounts are handled as stated in the agreement.

Refunds

Whether a refund is available, and in what amount, depends on the agreed terms and on the work delivered up to the cancellation date. Research findings and recommendations that have been delivered cannot be returned.

Advertising and third-party spend

Amounts paid to third-party platforms (for example advertising spend) are paid to that platform and are generally not refundable by SkillBridge.

How to request

Write to hello.manavlab@gmail.com with your name, the project and the reason for the request.
