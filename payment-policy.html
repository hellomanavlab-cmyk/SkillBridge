Agreed before work

Prices, what is included, billing schedule and due dates are stated in the proposal or agreement. Where a service has no published price, it is quoted individually (“Request a Quote”).

Currency

Billing is in Indian Rupees (INR) unless the agreement says otherwise. Any USD figures shown on the website are for reference only.

Third-party costs

Costs such as advertising spend, API usage, email or hosting providers may be separate and are paid to those providers unless the agreement states otherwise.

Payment methods

Payment methods are confirmed in the proposal. The website does not currently take online card payments.

Taxes and invoices

Invoices and any applicable taxes are handled as stated in the agreement.

Late or disputed payments

Contact us promptly about any payment question or dispute. We aim to resolve it before pausing any work.
