1. What It Is

Business Analysis is a free research report. It explains how a business appears online, identifies problems, and suggests practical improvement opportunities. It is powered by ManavLab.

2. What Information Is Needed

To prepare the report, please provide:

  • Business name;
  • Business location;
  • Website and social media links;
  • Products or services;
  • Main business problems;
  • Requirements;
  • Contact details.

3. How Research Is Performed

SkillBridge reviews the following sources:

  • Google Search
  • Google Maps
  • The business website
  • Instagram
  • Facebook
  • LinkedIn
  • Information you supply

The research is based on public information and on what you provide. We do not access private accounts or private areas of any platform.

4. What You Receive

You receive a written report with eight sections:

  1. Business Overview
  2. Online Presence Analysis
  3. Identified Problems
  4. Evidence and Research Sources
  5. Improvement Opportunities
  6. Recommended Solutions
  7. Suitable Professional Requirement, if needed
  8. Final Summary

The report is shared with you by the method agreed (WhatsApp or email).

5. The Eight Report Sections

No. Section What it covers
1 Business Overview What the business does, its offer and its location
2 Online Presence Analysis How the business appears on its website and social platforms
3 Identified Problems Issues found from the sources and your information
4 Evidence and Research Sources Where each finding came from
5 Improvement Opportunities Areas where the business could improve
6 Recommended Solutions Practical suggestions for each opportunity
7 Suitable Professional Requirement Whether a professional is needed, and what kind
8 Final Summary A short summary of the report

Business Analysis is a free service. Pending confirmation

7. Limitations

  • The report is based only on available information and may be incomplete.
  • The report is not a guarantee of business results.
  • The report is not legal, tax or financial advice.
  • Recommendations are suggestions. You decide whether to act on them.
