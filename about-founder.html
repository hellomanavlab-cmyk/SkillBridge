My learning journey

I am a young founder and builder, and I am still learning every day. I prefer building in public, testing carefully and fixing what does not work.

Why SkillBridge was created

Finding the right business partner or client is still hard for many small businesses and independent professionals. Information is scattered, contact details are hard to verify, and first conversations often start without context. SkillBridge was created to make that first step clearer and more careful: research first, be transparent about what is verified and what is not, and never promise results that depend on other people's decisions.

Mission

Help businesses and freelancers find relevant opportunities through honest research and respectful introductions.

Vision

Build SkillBridge into a dependable research service that helps freelancers, agencies and businesses discover relevant opportunities.
