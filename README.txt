SkillBridge website (final files)

HOW TO UPLOAD
1. Unzip this file.
2. Upload EVERYTHING inside the folder (index.html must be at the top level) to your hosting or GitHub Pages site.
3. Open the site address and check the homepage and the Policies page.

Nothing here is deployed automatically. Policy pages are drafts pending legal review and must be reviewed by a parent or guardian of the founder and a qualified Indian lawyer before publication.
Text marked "Pending confirmation" in the policy pages must be completed by the founder before publication.
