A. Lead Generation Service Policy

A.1 Overview

SkillBridge provides two lead services:

  1. Raw Leads: basic lead data.
  2. Verified Research Leads: leads that have gone through additional research and verification of the available information.

A.2 Raw Leads

  1. Fields. Business Name, Phone Number, Email (if available), Website (if available), Business Location.
  2. Not every field in every lead. Some leads will not contain every field.
  3. Free limit. The first 10 Raw Leads are free, one time per customer or business. The free limit does not restart with each new order.
  4. Price after the free limit. ₹99 per 10 Raw Leads.
  5. Full payment before transfer. Paid Raw Leads are transferred only after full payment is complete.

A.3 Verified Research Leads

  1. Price. ₹799 per 10 Verified Research Leads.
  2. Additional research and verification. Verified Research Leads have gone through additional research and verification of the available information. The specific activities are listed below in A.4.
  3. Meaning of “verified”. “Verified” means that the listed information has been researched and checked against the sources in A.4. It does not mean that the contact is interested, ready to buy, or will respond. It does not mean that every field is complete.
  4. Full payment before transfer. Verified Research Leads are transferred only after full payment is complete.

A.4 Research Methodology

  1. Confirmed research activity. Both lead services involve research of information available from public sources, and from information supplied by the business where relevant.
  2. Sources. Google Search, Google Maps, business websites, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. Pending confirmation

A.5 Delivery

  1. Leads are delivered by the method agreed at order (WhatsApp or email).
  2. Paid leads are delivered after full payment.
  3. Delivery timelines are targets. Pending confirmation

A.6 Pricing Summary

Service Price
Raw Leads First 10 free (one time per customer/business). Then ₹99 per 10.
Verified Research Leads ₹799 per 10 leads

A.7 Lead Limitations

  1. Lead information may be incomplete, outdated or incorrect.
  2. Information comes from third-party and public sources that SkillBridge does not control.
  3. Duplicate or incorrect records may be reported for correction under Section D.

A.8 No Guarantee Policy (applies to both lead services)

SkillBridge does not guarantee any of the following for either Raw Leads or Verified Research Leads:

  • Customer responses;
  • Buyer interest;
  • Meetings;
  • Sales;
  • Conversions;
  • Revenue;
  • Business growth;
  • Successful projects.
