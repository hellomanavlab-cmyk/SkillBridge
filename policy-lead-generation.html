1. Introduction and purpose

This Policy explains the lead generation services that SkillBridge may offer, what each service includes and does not include, how research is carried out, and the limitations that apply. It should be read with the Terms and Conditions (SB-TC-01) and the Privacy Policy (SB-PP-02).

Lead generation services are research and introduction services. They do not guarantee interest, sales, meetings or conversions.

2. Service tiers

SkillBridge currently offers two types of lead, described below. Each is different in what has been checked and what has been expressed by the prospect. They must not be confused with one another.

2.1 Raw Business Leads

A Raw Business Lead is a research-based business prospect. It includes:

  • the business name, category and location;
  • available business contact information found in relevant public sources;
  • source references and the date of research;
  • a short note of the reason the business appeared relevant to the research brief.

A Raw Business Lead is not a confirmed interested buyer. It has not been checked for interest, and there is no guarantee that the business wishes to buy, will respond or will purchase anything.

2.2 Verified Research Leads

A Verified Research Lead is a research-based report about a business. It may include:

  • a profile of the business and its relevant activities, based on public information;
  • available business contact details, checked for the date stated;
  • possible problems or opportunities identified from the available information;
  • suggested solutions or areas for improvement;
  • a recommended approach strategy;
  • the research date, verification status, sources and methodology.

Each report distinguishes confirmed facts from research-based observations. A Verified Research Lead is a research report and not a guaranteed customer. It does not confirm that the business has a problem, or that it is interested in buying, unless that has been confirmed by reliable evidence or expressed by the business.

3. Client requirements and research scope

Before research starts, SkillBridge agrees the scope with the client. The scope may include:

  • the type of business or industry to be researched;
  • the geographic area;
  • the number of leads or reports to be delivered;
  • the information fields required;
  • any exclusions, such as businesses already known to the client;
  • the time period for delivery, if agreed.

The agreed scope is recorded in the applicable quotation or order confirmation. Changes to scope may affect the timing and charges, and will be agreed in writing before they take effect.

4. Lead quantity and delivery scope

The number of leads or reports, the information included and the delivery method are as stated in the applicable quotation or order confirmation. SkillBridge does not guarantee that a specific number of leads will be found, or that every lead will meet the client’s criteria, where the available information does not support that.

Where fewer leads are found than requested, SkillBridge will tell the client and explain the reason where practical. Any adjustment to charges is covered by the Refund and Cancellation Policy (SB-RC-05).

5. Sources and research methodology

Research is carried out using publicly available information and any information the client has supplied. Each research record keeps the source reference and the date of research, so that the information can be checked.

SkillBridge does not access private systems, bypass access controls, or obtain information by deception. SkillBridge does not use information in a way that breaches a source’s terms or applicable law.

Where a Verified Research Lead includes a recommended approach, the recommendation is based on the information available at the time and is a suggestion only. The client decides whether to act on it.

6. Data accuracy limitations

Public information can change or be incomplete. Business contact details may be out of date, and sources may not reflect the current position of the business. SkillBridge does not guarantee that research data is accurate, complete or current.

Research-based observations are not confirmed facts. Where a report includes an observation, it is labelled as such. A client should check important information before relying on it.

7. No guarantee of sales, meetings or conversions

SkillBridge does not guarantee any sale, meeting, reply, project, customer, conversion or revenue from any lead or report. The outcome of any contact between a client and a prospect depends on those parties, not on SkillBridge.

8. Consent and contact-sharing rules

SkillBridge will not pass a prospect’s private contact details to a client for the purpose of direct contact unless the prospect has given consent for that connection, or the information is already public and is used in accordance with applicable law and the Privacy Policy (SB-PP-02).

Publicly available business contact information found in research is not automatically treated as consent for unsolicited contact. The client is responsible for how it uses any contact information it receives, including compliance with applicable law on electronic communication and marketing.

9. Report delivery

Reports and lead lists are delivered digitally to the client’s registered contact email or through another agreed method. Delivery is treated as complete when the material is sent to the agreed contact.

10. Revisions and limitations

Where a revision is included in the agreed scope, SkillBridge will make reasonable corrections to the report, such as correcting an error in source information, or clarifying a section of the report. Revisions that change the agreed scope are treated as a change to the service and may be chargeable as agreed in writing.

A revision does not change the limitations described in this Policy, and does not guarantee any outcome.

11. Service charges as agreed in the applicable quotation

The current charges for lead generation services are: Raw Business Leads are free. Verified Research Leads are ₹699 for 10 leads. For 15 and 25 leads, the charge is a Custom Quote. The charges that apply to any order are set out in the applicable quotation or order confirmation.

Any referral commission or other commercial arrangement must be separately agreed in writing. No such arrangement applies unless it is stated in writing.

12. No guarantee of results

SkillBridge does not promise guaranteed results from any lead generation service. Each service provides research information, which the client uses at its own discretion and responsibility.
