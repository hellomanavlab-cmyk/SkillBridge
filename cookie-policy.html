1. What we use

  • Cookie choice: a value saved in your browser's local storage (key: sb_cookie_consent) that records whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies. It is needed to remember your choice.
  • Session flags: short flags in your browser session (sessionStorage) so the introduction and the Diwali greeting do not repeat during the same visit. These are not sent to us.
  • Third-party requests: Google Fonts is loaded for typefaces. Your browser contacts Google, which may receive your IP address.

2. What we do not use

  • No analytics or tracking tools, such as Google Analytics or advertising pixels.
  • No login or account cookies on this public website.
  • No advertising cookies.

3. Your choice

A banner lets you accept or reject optional cookies. We currently use no optional cookies, so the choice has no effect on what we load. Rejecting does not affect the site's basic function. Clicking the Cookie Policy link on the banner is not treated as consent.

4. Changing your choice

Use the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer of any page to review or change your choice at any time.

5. If this changes

If we ever add analytics or other optional tools, they will stay switched off until you accept optional cookies, and this policy will be updated first.
