C. Professional Connection Policy

C.1 Purpose

Professional Connection introduces business owners to suitable professionals and freelancers, with permission from both sides.

C.2 Free Service and Zero Commission

  1. Professional Connection is completely free for business owners and for professionals.
  2. There is no introduction fee.
  3. SkillBridge takes no commission. This includes no percentage of project value and no commission after project completion.

C.3 Business Owner Process

  1. The business owner submits a requirement.
  2. SkillBridge identifies a possibly suitable professional.
  3. SkillBridge asks the business owner for permission before any introduction.
  4. If the business owner agrees, the introduction is made.

C.4 Professional Process

  1. The professional shares a profile or details of skills.
  2. SkillBridge asks the professional for permission before sharing the profile with a business owner.
  3. If the professional agrees, the profile is shared as part of an agreed introduction.

C.5 Introduction and Permission

  1. No introduction is made without permission from both the business owner and the professional.
  2. Permission may be withdrawn before the introduction is made.

C.6 Sharing Professional Profiles

  1. Profiles are shared only as far as the professional has agreed.
  2. Skills are described as the professional provides them.

C.7 Responsibilities of Both Parties

  • The business owner and the professional are each responsible for their own agreement, payments and project execution.
  • SkillBridge is not a party to any project agreement.

C.8 Project Execution Responsibility

Project scope, timelines, quality and payments are agreed directly between the business owner and the professional. SkillBridge is not responsible for project execution.

C.9 Skills

Professional skills are not verified unless a verification system is implemented and described on the Website. Pending confirmation

C.10 No Guarantee

SkillBridge does not guarantee any project, work, earnings or outcome from an introduction.
