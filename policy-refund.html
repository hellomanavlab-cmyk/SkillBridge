D. Refund & Cancellation Policy

D.1 Scope

This policy applies to paid Raw Leads and paid Verified Research Leads. Free services (the first 10 Raw Leads, Business Analysis and Professional Connection) have no payment to refund.

D.2 Cancellation Requests

  1. You may request cancellation of a paid order by contacting SkillBridge by email or WhatsApp.
  2. Cancellation window and refund eligibility for paid orders: Pending confirmation.

D.3 Duplicate Payments

If you pay more than once for the same order, the duplicate amount will be refunded or adjusted. Refund method: Pending confirmation.

D.4 Incorrect or Duplicate Lead Delivery

  1. If a delivered lead is incorrect or duplicated, tell us the record and the issue.
  2. We will correct or replace the affected record and confirm the correction to you.

D.5 Incomplete Delivery

  1. If a paid order is incomplete, tell us what is missing.
  2. We will complete the missing part, or refund the unused portion, as set out in Section D.7.

D.6 Replacement or Correction

Corrections and replacements are made on request, with the details of the issue.

D.7 Refund Assessment

  1. Refund decisions are assessed case by case.
  2. We consider the work already done, the proof of the issue you provide, and the delivery status.
  3. We will tell you the decision and the reasons.

D.8 Refund Processing

Refund method and timeline: Pending confirmation.

D.9 Statutory Rights

Nothing in this policy removes any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
