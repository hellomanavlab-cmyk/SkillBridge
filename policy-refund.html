Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellations, refunds and adjustments are handled.

1. Introduction and scope

This Policy explains how cancellations, refunds and related adjustments are handled for paid services on the SkillBridge platform. Paid services include Premium membership and lead generation services. The Policy should be read with the Terms and Conditions (SB-TC-01) and the Lead Generation Service Policy (SB-LG-04).

Each request is assessed on its own facts. This Policy does not remove or limit any right that you have under applicable Indian law, including consumer protection law.

2. Enquiry and order confirmation

An enquiry is not a paid order. A paid order is confirmed when SkillBridge sends an order confirmation or quotation that has been accepted in writing, and the agreed payment has been made or arranged.

The order confirmation or quotation states the service, scope, charges and any delivery terms that apply.

3. Cancellation requests

You may request cancellation of a paid order by contacting skillbridge.manavlab@gmail.com with your order details. SkillBridge will confirm receipt and tell you how the request will be assessed.

A cancellation request is assessed on the basis of whether work has started, the stage the work has reached, and any costs that SkillBridge has already incurred.

4. Work started versus work not started

PositionHow the request is treated
Work has not startedWhere work has not started, a full or substantial refund may be considered, less any non-refundable charges that were clearly stated before payment.
Work has startedWhere work has started, SkillBridge may refund the part of the charge that relates to work not yet done, less a fair amount reflecting work already completed or costs already incurred.
Work is completeWhere work has been delivered in full, a refund is generally not available unless the delivery was incorrect, incomplete or otherwise not as agreed (see Section 6).

SkillBridge will explain how it has calculated any refund or deduction.

5. Digital report delivery

Reports and lead lists are delivered digitally. Delivery is complete when the material is sent to the registered contact email, or through another method agreed in writing. Please check your delivered material promptly and tell SkillBridge of any problem.

6. Incorrect or incomplete delivery

If a delivered report or lead list is materially incorrect or incomplete compared with the agreed scope, you may tell SkillBridge in writing. SkillBridge will review the issue and, where appropriate, correct, complete or re-deliver the material.

Where SkillBridge cannot correct or complete the delivery to match the agreed scope, a refund or adjustment will be considered on a case-by-case basis.

A difference of opinion about the usefulness of a research observation, or the fact that a lead did not respond or convert, is not in itself a reason for a refund. Research observations are not guarantees (see Lead Generation Service Policy, SB-LG-04).

7. Duplicate payments

Where a payment has been made more than once for the same order, SkillBridge will refund the duplicate amount, after confirming the duplicate payment. This will be processed through the same method as the original payment, where practical.

8. Partial completion

Where part of an agreed service has been delivered, the client will be charged for the part that has been delivered, and a refund or adjustment may be considered for the balance. The amount is based on the proportion of the agreed scope that has been completed, and is confirmed in writing.

9. Refund assessment

All refund requests are assessed case by case. SkillBridge will consider the agreed scope, the stage of work, the delivery record, any costs already incurred, and the reason for the request.

SkillBridge does not apply fixed refund percentages in this Policy. Any refund, deduction or credit is confirmed in writing to the client before it is processed.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method where practical. Where that is not possible, SkillBridge will agree an alternative method with you in writing.

10. Premium membership cancellation

A Premium membership may be cancelled by contacting skillbridge.manavlab@gmail.com. SkillBridge will confirm receipt of the cancellation request in writing.

The cancellation and access conditions for Premium membership, including the effect of cancellation on Premium features, will be communicated in the applicable membership terms. Until those terms are published, SkillBridge will confirm the effect of any cancellation in writing.

A Premium membership that has been cancelled or lapsed does not affect the free basic listing, which remains subject to the Terms and Conditions and the Listing Policy. The treatment of the Premium badge after cancellation will be communicated in the applicable membership terms.

Refunds for Premium membership are assessed in the same way as other paid services under this Policy, based on the period used and the circumstances of the request.

11. Delays and exceptional situations

Where a delay arises from circumstances outside SkillBridge’s reasonable control, SkillBridge will inform you as soon as practicable and agree a revised timetable in writing. Where a delay affects the value of a service to you, SkillBridge will consider a refund or adjustment on a case-by-case basis.

SkillBridge does not impose arbitrary deadlines in this Policy. Where a deadline applies, it is stated in the order confirmation or quotation.

12. Communication and resolution

Refund and cancellation requests should be sent in writing to skillbridge.manavlab@gmail.com. SkillBridge will acknowledge each request, explain its assessment and tell you the outcome in writing.

If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may ask SkillBridge to review the decision. Nothing in this Policy prevents you from exercising any right under applicable law, including any right to seek redress through the relevant consumer authorities or courts in India.

13. Contact and policy updates

For cancellation or refund questions, contact SkillBridge at skillbridge.manavlab@gmail.com or visit https://hellomanavlab-cmyk.github.io/SkillBridge/.
